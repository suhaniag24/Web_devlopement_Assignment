GLA University

About

GLA University is a private university in Uttar Pradesh. It provides students with a supportive environment for academic learning, professional development and extracurricular activities.

Programs

GLA University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs in fields such as Computer Science, Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Biotechnology and other professional disciplines.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University includes modern classrooms, laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, student clubs and cultural activities. Students can participate in various events and activities along with their studies.

Admission
