Heavy Rain Brings Delhi-NCR to a Standstill

Gurugram Shifts Schools and Offices Online as Waterlogging Disrupts Daily Life

New Delhi, August 25, 2026: Heavy rain continued to lash Delhi-NCR on Tuesday after torrential rainfall the previous day caused widespread waterlogging and traffic congestion. The intense weather disrupted normal life across the region, with several roads and underpasses affected by accumulated rainwater. The situation became particularly serious in Gurugram, where authorities took measures to reduce traffic and protect residents.

In Gurugram, the District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) directed corporate offices, government departments and private educational institutions to switch to work-from-home and remote-learning arrangements. The decision was taken because heavy rainfall threatened to make major roads difficult to use. Civic agencies were also given an opportunity to carry out drainage clearing, de-watering operations and urgent road restoration work without additional traffic pressure.

The rainfall also caused major problems in several parts of Delhi. Waterlogging was reported around areas including ITO, Mathura Road, Connaught Place and other busy locations. According to the India Meteorological Department, Chhatarpur recorded 28 mm of rainfall between 8:30 am and 11:30 am on Monday, while Lodi Road recorded 24.6 mm during the same period. The continuing rain made commuting difficult for motorists, pedestrians and public transport users.

Authorities have urged residents to avoid unnecessary travel during periods of heavy rainfall and to remain alert to weather advisories. The disruption highlights the challenges faced by rapidly growing cities when heavy rain overwhelms drainage systems and transport networks. For students and office workers, the shift to online activities provides a safer alternative while authorities work to restore normal conditions.

The situation remains important for residents of Delhi-NCR because continued rainfall could lead to further waterlogging and traffic disruption. Emergency and civic teams are working to clear affected areas and restore road conditions. Residents should follow official instructions and check weather updates before travelling.
