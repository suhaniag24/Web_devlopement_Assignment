Academic Terms

Lecture
A class in which the teacher explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or coding tasks.
Tutorial
A session focused on practice, problem-solving and doubt clarification.
Internal Assessment
An evaluation conducted during the academic semester.
Practical
A hands-on examination or activity used to test practical knowledge.
