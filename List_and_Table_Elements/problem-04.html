How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Decide your budget.
  2. Choose a suitable processor.
  3. Select enough RAM for your work.
  4. Choose the required storage capacity.
  5. Check the display size and quality.
  6. Compare battery life.
  7. Check warranty and after-sales service.
  8. Choose the laptop that best matches your needs.

Important Laptop Terms

RAM
RAM is temporary memory used by a computer to run applications.
SSD
SSD is a fast storage device used to store files and applications.
CPU
CPU is the main processor responsible for executing instructions.
GPU
GPU handles graphics and is useful for gaming, design and AI workloads.
Refresh Rate
Refresh rate is the number of times a display updates its image per second.
Battery Capacity
Battery capacity indicates how much energy a laptop battery can store.
