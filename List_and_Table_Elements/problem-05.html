Rules and Guidelines

  • College ID card is mandatory for entry and verification.
  • Participants must reach the venue 15 minutes prior to the event schedule.
  • Any form of misconduct will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Decisions made by the judges will be final and binding.

Term Definitions

Open Event
An event open to participants from all departments and years.
Team Event
An event requiring a group of 2 or more participants.
Individual Event
An event contested by a single participant.
Registration
The formal process of enrolling for an event before the deadline.
Coordinator
The student or faculty member responsible for managing a specific event.
Final Round
The ultimate stage of a competition determining the winners.
