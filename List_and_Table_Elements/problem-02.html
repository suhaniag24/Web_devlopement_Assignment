Abert Pinto - Menu

Abert Pinto is a Goan-Portuguese themed restaurant in Ithum Galleria Mall, Alpha II, Greater Noida.It's not meant to be just a regular restaurant. The interiors, menu and presentation are built around a Goa- inspired narrative, so the ambience is a big part of the experience. Reviews also frequently mention the food, ambience, live music and service positively.

Menu

  • Starters

  • Tomato salad with burrata & ’nduja sauce – creamy, spicy, and vibrant.
  • Paneer tikka – marinated, grilled paneer with mint chutney.
  • Veg spring rolls – crisp rolls filled with sautéed vegetables.
  • Hara bhara kebab – spinach, peas, and potato patties.
  • Lentil croquettes with za’atar & labneh – crispy and aromatic.

  • Main Courses

    • Goan prawn curry – prawns in a coconut and tamarind sauce.
    • Chicken vindaloo – tender chicken in a spicy, tangy sauce.
    • Pork sorpotel – slow-cooked pork in a rich, spicy sauce.
    • Vegetable xacuti – mixed vegetables in a coconut and spice sauce.
    • Fish cafreal – marinated fish fillets in a green herb sauce.

  • Desserts

    • Bebinca – traditional Goan layered dessert made with coconut milk and eggs.
    • Serradura – Portuguese-style layered dessert with whipped cream and crushed biscuits.
    • Flan – creamy caramel custard with a hint of vanilla.
    • Chocolate mousse – rich and airy chocolate dessert.
