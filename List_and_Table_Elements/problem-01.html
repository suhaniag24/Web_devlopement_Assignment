Admission Overview

GLA University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Students can apply for admission by following the required admission procedure and meeting the eligibility criteria.

Eligibility

Students must fulfill the eligibility requirements for their chosen course. Valid educational documents are mandatory.

Application Process

The admission process should be completed in the following order:

  1. Submit the online application form.
  2. Upload the required documents.
  3. Appear for the entrance examination (if applicable).
  4. Attend the counseling session (if shortlisted).

Required Documents

The following documents may be required:

  • Class 10 Marksheet
  • Class 12 Marksheet
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
