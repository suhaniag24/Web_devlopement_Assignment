About the Expo

National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a technology and career event where students can explore companies, internships, workshops, certifications and placement opportunities.

The event will bring together students, technology companies, industry experts, recruiters and educators under one platform.

Registration is now open! Join the expo to discover your next career opportunity.

"The future belongs to those who prepare for it today."

Organizer's Message

Message from the Organizing Team

Our goal is to connect students with organizations and professionals who can help them understand the changing technology industry.

We encourage every participant to attend workshops, interact with professionals and explore career opportunities.

Connect with professionals | Explore career opportunities | Explore online courses

Speakers and Industry Experts

Technology Leaders

Industry professionals will discuss artificial intelligence, cloud computing, cybersecurity, software development and emerging technologies.

Career Experts

Career experts will guide students about resumes, interviews, internships, placements and professional networking.

Student Opportunities

Participants can interact with recruiters and learn about internships, projects, entry-level positions and training programs.

Expo Preparation Tips

  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Research the participating organizations.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and speakers.
  • Keep your student identification with you.
  • Attend workshops according to your interests.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your expo confirmation.
  3. Check the event schedule.
  4. Attend the expo and collect your visitor badge.
  5. Explore companies and career opportunities.
  6. Attend workshops and speaker sessions.
  7. Network with industry professionals.
  8. Follow up with organizations after the expo.

Career Terms

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals meet organizations and explore career opportunities.
Internship
A temporary professional experience that helps students gain practical skills.
Placement
An opportunity for a student to obtain employment through an organization or institution.
Workshop
An interactive session designed to teach practical skills.
Certification
An official recognition that a person has completed a particular course or skill assessment.
Networking
The process of building professional relationships with people in an industry.
