Destination Overview

Welcome to Agra

Agra is one of India's most famous historical destinations. Located on the banks of the Yamuna River, it is home to the magnificent Taj Mahal and several other Mughal-era monuments.

Agra is perfect for visitors interested in history, architecture, photography, food and culture. A short trip can cover many of its major attractions.

Travel Tips

Useful Advice

  • Wear comfortable footwear because many monuments require walking.
  • Carry drinking water and sun protection.
  • Start early to avoid crowds at major attractions.
  • Keep your valuables secure in crowded areas.
  • Check monument opening days before travelling.
