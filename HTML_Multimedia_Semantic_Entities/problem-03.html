Recruitment Stages

Application Workflow

  1. Online Application Submission
  2. Initial Profile Screening
  3. Technical Assessment / Interview
  4. HR Round & Culture Fitment

Recruitment Stage Definitions

Screening
Initial evaluation of candidate resume, background, and alignment with open role requirements.
Technical Round
In-depth technical evaluation, live coding, domain-specific problem solving, or portfolio presentation.
HR Round
Discussion covering company culture fitment, soft skills, compensation expectations, and policies.
Offer
Formal job offer extend with detailed compensation breakdown, onboarding schedule, and terms.
