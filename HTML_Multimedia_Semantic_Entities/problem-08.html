Understanding Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps you safely store money while earning interest. It is suitable for emergency funds, short-term goals and regular savings.

If you deposit ₹50,000 at an annual interest rate of 6%, the simple interest for one year is: ₹50,000 × 6 ÷ 100 = ₹3,000.

"The goal is not simply to save money, but to make every rupee work wisely."

Types of Accounts

  • Savings Account
  • Current Account
  • Fixed Deposit Account
  • Recurring Deposit Account

Understanding Credit and Debit Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to spend money directly from your bank account. You should keep track of your balance and transactions.

Credit Card

A credit card allows you to borrow money up to a specific credit limit. Paying the bill on time can help avoid additional charges.

Important: Always understand the interest rate, annual fee and repayment terms before using credit.

Understanding Loans

What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a financial institution that is repaid over an agreed period. The borrower generally pays principal + interest.

Common loans include home loans, education loans, personal loans and vehicle loans.

Steps to Open an Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank and account.
  2. Complete the application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Complete the verification process.
  5. Receive your account details.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on a principal amount.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or investment is maintained.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your income regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Compare interest rates before taking a loan.
  • Pay bills and EMIs on time.
  • Keep an emergency fund.
