Student Terms & Rules

Welcome to our institution. All students are expected to follow the rules and guidelines mentioned below.

General Rules

1. Students must maintain respectful behavior towards teachers, staff members, and other students.

2. Students must carry their identity card while attending classes and other institutional activities.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75%.
Attendance is important for academic progress and eligibility for examinations.

4. Students should attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Examination

5. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the institution.

6. Any form of unfair means during an examination is strictly prohibited.
Students found violating examination rules may face disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

7. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

8. Students must submit their own work and avoid copying from other students.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or may not be accepted.

Discipline

9. Students must maintain discipline inside classrooms and throughout the campus.

10. Damaging institutional property is strictly prohibited.
Students are responsible for taking care of classroom and campus facilities.

Important Notice

All students are required to follow these rules and regulations.

Failure to follow the rules may result in appropriate disciplinary action.
