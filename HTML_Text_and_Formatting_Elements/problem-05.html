Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is represented by the chemical formula H2O.

Carbon dioxide is represented by CO2.

Oxygen gas is represented by O2.

Sulfuric acid has the formula H2SO4.

Sodium chloride has the formula NaCl.

A chemical formula shows the elements and the number of atoms present in a compound.

Atoms are the basic building blocks of matter.

Physics

.

The area of a square is measured in m2.

The volume of a cube is measured in m3.

The speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Speed is the distance travelled divided by the time taken.

Speed = Distance / Time

Gravity is the force that attracts objects toward Earth.

Biology

The chemical formula for glucose is C6H12O6.

Water is essential for all living organisms and has the formula H2O.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their food using sunlight.

Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen

The human body contains approximately 3 × 1013 cells.
