Hiring Process

Our transparent hiring workflow is designed to evaluate skills and ensure mutual fit.

  1. Application Submission & Initial Review
  2. Pre-screening Assessment
  3. Technical Assessment / Interview
  4. Culture & HR Round
  5. Formal Offer Presentation

Hiring Stage Details

Screening
Initial review of your resume and portfolio background by the recruitment team.
Technical Round
Hands-on coding, system architecture evaluation, or domain-specific problem solving.
HR Round
Discussion surrounding behavioral scenarios, culture compatibility, and expectations.
Offer
Finalization of job offer details, compensation terms, and onboarding plan.
