Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 brings together universities, colleges, companies, training organizations and career experts under one platform.

The event aims to help participants understand career opportunities, higher education options, internships and placement pathways.

The fair will be held on 15 October 2026 at the National Convention Centre, New Delhi.

Objectives of the Fair

  • Connect students with universities and employers.
  • Provide information about higher education programs.
  • Introduce participants to career opportunities.
  • Help students understand internship and placement options.
  • Provide guidance from experienced career professionals.
"Choose a career that allows you to learn, grow and contribute meaningfully to society."

Preparation Guide

Tips Before Attending

  • Research organizations you want to meet.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Prepare questions about courses and career opportunities.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your academic documents available if required.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete check-in.
  4. Attend orientation and selected sessions.
  5. Visit participating organizations.
  6. Interact with career counselors and representatives.
  7. Attend networking and career sessions.
  8. Collect participation certificate.

Important Preparation Notice

Participants should arrive at least 30 minutes before their selected session.

Keep your registration confirmation and a valid photo identification document available at the venue.

Information & FAQs

Who Can Attend?

School students, college students, graduates, job seekers, educators and career changers can attend the fair.

Is Registration Required?

Yes. Participants should complete the online registration form before attending the event.

What Should I Bring?

Participants should carry their registration confirmation, identification and relevant academic or career documents.

Important Terms

Internship
A temporary professional learning opportunity that provides practical experience.
Placement
An opportunity for students or graduates to obtain employment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training program combining practical work and learning.
