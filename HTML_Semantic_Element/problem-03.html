How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select your preferred department.
  2. Choose a suitable doctor and consultation time.
  3. Fill in your patient information.
  4. Provide the reason for your visit.
  5. Read and accept the consent information.
  6. Submit the appointment request.
Good healthcare begins with timely attention, clear communication and informed decisions.

Important Patient Instructions

  • Carry previous medical reports when applicable.
  • Arrive a few minutes before your scheduled appointment.
  • Inform the clinic if you need to reschedule.
  • Keep your appointment details available during your visit.

For emergencies, contact your local emergency medical service instead of using this appointment form.
